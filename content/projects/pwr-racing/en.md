## About

I develop the website of the oldest Formula Student team in Poland as part of KN Solvro. The team manages cars, news, partners and recruitment from the CMS, and I am the top committer on the main branch.

## What I built

- Payload CMS integration with car and news collections and S3 storage
- A 3D model of the car in Three.js and React Three Fiber
- The car page, recruitment pages and team stats on the About page
- A new navbar, SEO, sitemap and analytics
- Upgrades to Next.js 15 and 16
