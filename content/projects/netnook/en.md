## Tech stack

- Next.js: frontend and backend in one place
- Payload CMS: content and product management
- Stripe: secure payments
- MongoDB with built-in vector search

## Key features

- User accounts with roles
- Cart for signed-in users and guests
- Vector search for products
- Full payment flow with webhooks
- Pro subscription and saved payment methods
- Products managed in Payload and synced to Stripe
