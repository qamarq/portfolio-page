## About

Built at the Google DeepMind × AI Tinkerers Hackathon in Warsaw on 28 March 2026. Gotownik made the final six out of 30 projects.

- Fridge scan and recipe research
- A real-time cooking agent with voice and video
- Ingredient orders through Wolt
- A MacBook trackpad used as a kitchen scale, through a companion SwiftUI app

## My part

- Video chat with Gemini Live
- The cooking screen with steps, timers and ingredient weighing
- A tool that lets the agent change steps mid-cook
