Our entry for BEST Hacks 2025, a 24-hour hackathon at Wrocław University of Science and Technology themed “Safe Society”.

- Map of shelters and AEDs with clustering
- Hazard reports from users
- Layers for air quality, fires and water levels
- A “Backpack” section
