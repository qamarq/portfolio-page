## better-auth-usos

Adds sign-in through USOS, the student system used by Polish universities, over OAuth 1.0a.

- Over 2,000 downloads on npm since March 2026
- Listed among the community plugins in the [Better Auth docs](https://better-auth.com/docs/plugins/community-plugins)
- Used in production by Solvro Planer and HRownik
- [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/better-auth-usos) · [GitHub](https://github.com/qamarq/better-auth-usos)

## better-auth-evp

A plugin for Chrome’s experimental Email Verification Protocol: sign-in without a password or a code, with an automatic fallback to any other method.

- 1,400+ downloads in its first month
- Also listed in the Better Auth docs and used as a fast sign-in path in Solvro Planer
- [Live demo](https://evp.kamilmarczak.pl) · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/better-auth-evp) · [GitHub](https://github.com/qamarq/better-auth-evp)
