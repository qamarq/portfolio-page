## About

Better USOS puts the university system in a fast, native app. It started in 2024 as a Java app and in 2026 I rewrote it from scratch in Jetpack Compose with Material 3 Expressive. It is coming to Google Play soon.

## Key features

- A home screen with upcoming classes, pending surveys and the academic calendar
- A schedule calendar with days off, rector’s days and an option to treat lecture-only days as free
- Live class notifications: a reminder 5 minutes before with the room and building, then the class progress
- Notifications about new grades
- Student ID validity, index number and fields of study in one place
- Payments that show what is paid and what is still due
- Lecturer search and schedule widgets

## Wear OS

- ECTS average and grades on the watch
- Today’s plan and Wear OS tiles
- Pairing with the phone app

## Tech stack

- Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with Material 3 Expressive
- Wear Compose, Tiles and ProtoLayout on the watch
- Glance for home screen widgets
